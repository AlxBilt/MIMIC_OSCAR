CAN BUS
----------------------------------------------------------
CAN1 (Engine)
  Pin 0   RX1
  Pin 1   TX1

CAN2 (Vehicle)
  Pin 22  RX2
  Pin 23  TX2

CAN3 (Slave PCM)
  Pin 30  RX3
  Pin 31  TX3

----------------------------------------------------------
